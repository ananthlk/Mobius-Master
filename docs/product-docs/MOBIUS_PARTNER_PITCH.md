# Mobius: What Community Mental Health Centers Need to Know

## The Problem (Quantified)

Community mental health centers (CMHCs) are being quietly displaced.

The data from Florida's Medicaid behavioral health market (2019–2024) shows it clearly:
- **$144M–$217M in annual value** the network is leaving on the table
- **Patient leakage:** $60–125M (patients slip away before intake completes)
- **Admin overspend:** ~$66M (34 separate back offices duplicating work)
- **Clinician churn:** $10–18M (centers can't afford to keep staff while chasing denials)
- **Rate gap:** $7.8M (centers don't know they're being paid 40% less than peers for the same service)

No single CMHC can fix this alone. The solution had to be **network-scale**.
