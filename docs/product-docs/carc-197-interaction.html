What the shape of this run demonstrates

The machine's best answer was "do not appeal". Every branch closed on evidence, zero appeal levels were consumed, and it finished with 10 days to spare. A system that can only produce appeals would have filed one — on B3, at 0.28, against facts that refute it.
Closures accumulate; the Drafter cannot re-argue them. Pass 2 explicitly forbids reopening a closed branch except as a challenge with new evidence. Without that rule the same model re-litigates B1 every pass and the case never converges.
B2 was decidable from the claim alone, and we paid for retrieval anyway. 97 > 90 needed no document — only the rulebook's number, which we did not hold. The Recorder files that as playbook feedback: a cheap precondition gate would have closed B2 in pass 1 and saved a dispatch. The loop improving the playbook is the same loop.
The Judge never saw a weight, and the Arbiter never saw a quote it had not been given. B3 carried the second-highest prior and was refuted on a single field. Had the Judge known it was favoured, "partial" would have been under pressure to read as "proven".
The 90-day retro window, the manual page and the appeal deadline are drawn from Sunshine Health facts this loop sourced and its critic accepted; the claim, the member, the weights and the clinical fields are illustrative.
